🧠 Decisions Made For You — Read These

10k Steps → Split Never a separate "go walk" block 2k walk to/from gym · 3k post-lunch walk · 5k evening walk. Baked into transitions so it doesn't add time.
Typing Practice 15 min after breakfast, every day Use keybr.com or monkeytype. At 7 days/week you hit 85+ WPM in 90 days. Non-negotiable.
GitHub Commits 1 commit minimum, daily Even if it's a README update or comment fix. Green streak = credibility to recruiters. Tied to project block.
Job Search Timing Moved to 9:30–11:30 AM Freshest brain is post-gym. Job apps, LinkedIn, cold emails done before lunch — not as an afterthought.
Certifications One at a time. AWS first. AWS Cloud Practitioner (Month 1–2) → AWS Solutions Architect (3–4) → ML Specialization (5–6). Done at 9:30 PM.
Study Rebalanced DSA 1.5h · ML 45min · Systems 30min ML cut by 15 min to protect energy. Quality beats time. Sunday adds mock interviews + LinkedIn audit.
Deload Week Every 4th week: half everything No gym. 1.5h study. Job search only. This is what makes 6 months possible — most people burn out at month 2.
Personal Projects Saturdays only, 10 AM–1 PM One project at a time until deployed. GitHub commit mandatory at end. No "half-built" repos.

⚖ Pros & Cons

✅ Pros

  • 🧠Job search in the morning = freshest brain on the most important task
  • 🚶10k steps baked into transitions — zero extra time added to your day
  • ⌨️15 min typing daily compounds: 85+ WPM in 90 days without effort
  • 🟢Daily GitHub commit builds visible proof of discipline to every recruiter
  • 📜3 certs in 6 months is achievable and each one opens new interview doors
  • 🔄Built-in deload weeks = you won't quit. Most people crash at month 2 without this
  • 🎯One project at a time until deployed = no half-built repos on GitHub
  • 😴Sleep protected. Everything else flows from this

⚠ Cons

  • 📦Very packed — zero room for unexpected college workload spikes. Have a fallback: drop cert study first, keep job search + gym
  • 💪5 days lifting is high volume. Without eating enough protein and deload weeks, you'll feel it by month 2
  • 📬Job search takes 3–6 months minimum to see results. Stay patient in month 1 and 2
  • 🤯Three subjects (DSA + ML + Systems) per day can feel like too much on hard gym days. Solution: on heavy leg day, drop ML
  • 🤝Social life is limited to evenings only — can feel isolating. Fix: protect Saturday evenings as full social time
  • 🔋The schedule demands consistent sleep. One late night cascades: fix it same day, not "I'll catch up tomorrow"
⚠ Health & Recovery Reminders

If You Feel Dizzy or Drained

Banana + peanut butter on the walk to gym — every single day, not just when dizzy Hydrate 500ml before gym, 500ml after — set phone alarms if you forget If energy crashes mid-week: drop cert study that night, sleep by 10 PM Every 4th week is deload — no gym, halved study, job search only. Schedule it now If you miss a day: skip it completely, don't double up the next day. Just resume
